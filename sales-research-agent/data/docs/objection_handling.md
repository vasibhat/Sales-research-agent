# Objection handling

## "We already have SAP, it does this."

SAP stores invoices but does not read them. InvoiceFlow does the reading and matching, then posts into SAP. The retail case study customer was also on SAP.

## "It is too expensive."

Show the savings: early-payment discounts and stopped duplicate payments usually cover the cost within 6 to 9 months. Offer a 30-day pilot on one business unit.

## "Our invoices are messy, AI will make mistakes."

Every invoice gets a confidence score. Low-confidence invoices go to a person for review. Customers start at around 70% no-touch and reach 80%+ within 3 months.

## "Data security."

Data stays in Indian data centres. We are ISO 27001 certified and support single sign-on.
