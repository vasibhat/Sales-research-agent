# Ideal customer profile

## Who we sell to

Mid-size and large companies in India, UAE and Singapore with high invoice volume: retail, FMCG, manufacturing, logistics, pharmaceuticals, e-commerce and automotive.

Best fit: 1,000+ employees, 2,000+ supplier invoices a month, already using an ERP such as SAP or Oracle.

## Buying signals

- New funding, IPO plans or an acquisition (more invoices, more pressure on finance)
- New plants, warehouses or stores (expansion)
- A new CFO or finance head
- Hiring many accounts payable or finance staff
- ERP upgrade or digital transformation project

## Poor fit

Companies under 200 employees, or companies with very few suppliers.
