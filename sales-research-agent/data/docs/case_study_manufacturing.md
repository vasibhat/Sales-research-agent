# Case study: auto parts manufacturer

Customer: an auto parts maker with 4 plants and 2,800 employees (name kept private).

## Problem

Each plant had its own accounts payable team. Duplicate invoices were paid twice, and GST input credit was lost because purchase records did not match GSTR-2B.

## Solution

InvoiceFlow across all 4 plants with one central approval desk, plus GST Recon.

## Result

- INR 48 lakh of duplicate payments stopped in 6 months
- INR 1.3 crore of missing input tax credit recovered
- Month-end close faster by 5 days
