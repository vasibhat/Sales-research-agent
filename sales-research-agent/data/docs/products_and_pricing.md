# Products and pricing

LedgerLeaf Technologies is a sample company used in this project. Replace these documents with your own company's documents.

## InvoiceFlow

InvoiceFlow reads supplier invoices (PDF, scan, email) and pulls out invoice number, GSTIN, line items, tax and totals. It matches each invoice with the purchase order and goods receipt (3-way match) and sends approved invoices to the ERP.

Best for companies that get more than 2,000 supplier invoices a month. Works with SAP, Oracle, Tally and Microsoft Dynamics.

Price: from INR 4 per invoice, minimum INR 50,000 per month. Setup fee INR 3,00,000.

## PayablesHub

PayablesHub is the accounts payable dashboard: approvals, payment runs, vendor ageing and early-payment discounts. Most customers buy it together with InvoiceFlow.

Price: INR 1,500 per user per month.

## GST Recon

GST Recon matches purchase records with GSTR-2B every month and flags missing input tax credit.

Price: INR 25,000 per GSTIN per year.
