# Case study: national retail chain

Customer: a retail chain with 300 stores and about 4,500 suppliers (name kept private).

## Problem

The finance team of 40 people typed 60,000 supplier invoices a month into SAP by hand. Invoices took 18 days to approve, and the chain lost early-payment discounts.

## Solution

InvoiceFlow with 3-way match against store goods receipts, plus PayablesHub for approvals. Live in 10 weeks.

## Result

- 82% of invoices processed with no human touch
- Approval time down from 18 days to 4 days
- INR 2.1 crore saved in the first year from early-payment discounts
- 25 finance staff moved to vendor management work
