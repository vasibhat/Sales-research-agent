# Case study: logistics and warehousing company

Customer: a logistics company with 60 warehouses and a large fleet of transport vendors (name kept private).

## Problem

Freight and warehouse vendor invoices came in many formats. Disputes over rates were common, and vendors complained about late payment.

## Solution

InvoiceFlow with contract rate checks for freight invoices, and PayablesHub vendor portal so vendors can see payment status.

## Result

- Rate mismatches caught automatically: INR 75 lakh saved in a year
- Vendor queries down by 60%
- Payment cycle cut from 45 days to 21 days
